How to read the numbers

Every statement in this exhibition, on the walls and in the audio guide, carries a source number and a page. Words in quotation marks are the source’s own. Many sources are in Japanese; where they are, the English on screen is a paraphrase. The full ledger, one row per claim with the passage it rests on, is public: claims ledger.

Object titles, dates and materials are the museums’ own records. They are cited as records, not as scholarship.

Pictures and sound

Photographs: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access (CC0), each credited on its label. The galleries, the cloth, the cutting, the sewing and the dressed figure were built by hand in Blender from straight panels measured in the sources, and rendered for this exhibition. Room tone and the sounds of cloth are synthesised in code.

The audio guide uses a synthetic voice (ElevenLabs “Alice”, via Venice AI). Captions are on by default.

Real photographs and hand-built 3D; nothing generated. Narration: synthetic voice (Venice AI).
