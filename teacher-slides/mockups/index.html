Example 1

Statistical or not?

A short story about two questions

Mint asks herself: “What time did I wake up?” → only one answer: 6:15.
Then she asks her whole class: “What time did you wake up?” → the answers are all different.
Some of the answers
FriendPloyTonMintBankFahEarth
Woke up6:006:307:156:305:456:45
whole class (17 students) · spread 5:45 – 7:30
Shortcut
Ask: could different people give different answers? Yes → it is a statistical question. Answer: Mint's class question is statistical
