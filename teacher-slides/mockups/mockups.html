Lesson 1 · Statistical questions

Is it a statistical question?

“What time did I wake up today?”
6:15
One answer → not statistical
“What time did the students in our class wake up today?”
Many different answers → statistical ✓

A statistical question expects answers that vary, so we must collect data to answer it.
