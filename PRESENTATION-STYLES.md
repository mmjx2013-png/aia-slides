# Presentation styles: the owner's vocabulary

When the owner asks for a presentation "แบบที่หนึ่ง" (type 1) or "แบบที่สอง" (type 2), they mean:

## Type 1: the target ("perfect")
`reference/from-brand-to-place-cinematic.html`
- Cinematic photographic deck on a fixed 1920x1080 stage scaled to fit.
- Full-bleed real photographs with slow drift (scale 1.05, about ±1% over 28 s), dark or light veils and grain.
- Persistent paper "label" cards are the continuity device. A card grows into a full panel, slides to a column, shrinks back into a card and flips in 3D (rotateY 180°) to reveal the answer on its back. Text fades out before a card moves and back in after; text never slides.
- Split screens put two photos side by side with clip-path wipes.
- One easing curve, cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.24,1); 0.4 to 0.7 s; text staggers in at 70 ms.
- Beats inside a slide: quick check, then the answer. Credits slide up as the final beat.
- Big serif headlines, very little text, captions carry caveats, every claim sourced.

## Type 2: acceptable, the minimum standard
"Heat Absorption / Heat Emission" classroom deck:
- Bold condensed uppercase sans headlines, flat vector diagrams (sun rays, sample plates), spectral charts.
- Big numbers (e.g. 98.1%, 0.95), split black/cream layouts, mono labels, one concept per slide.
- Good, but the owner prefers type 1.

## Never
A scroll website that turns into a wall of text. Slides, not pages.

## Lessons for next time (the owner's feedback, 24 Nov 2026)
- **Few shots, extreme detail.** Model and render only a few 3D images, but make each one look like a real photograph. Then build the explanation around those images. The 3D replaces AI image generation: we make the pictures ourselves.
- **Too perfect reads as fake.** The kimono 3D sat alone in the middle of a tidy, empty room: unmarked wood, everything aligned. Real places have clutter, wear, scratches, asymmetry and things slightly out of place.
- **Light must look like real light, not a simulation.** Grade like a camera: rolled-off highlights, real exposure falloff, lens character, grain. Try a colour grade first.
- **No film mode next time.** Type 1 slides only.
- **Downloaded models are fine** when the licence allows it (CC0 / CC BY, credited): Poly Haven, Smithsonian Open Access 3D (real scanned museum objects), Sketchfab (CC filters, needs an API token), BlenderKit and Fab, each checked one by one.
