Watch the cloth, not the labels.

It leaves the roll at one width, and nobody will ever cut a curve into it.

A few straight cuts across. That is all the cutting there is.

Then the rectangles are sewn back together, edge to edge.

Lift it by the shoulders and the whole garment is there.

Lay it down again, and it is as flat as the bolt it came from.

Nothing in it has been shaped to a body. No dart, no tuck, no curve.

So here is the puzzle of this exhibition. If the body is not in the cloth, where is it?
