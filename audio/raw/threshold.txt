Welcome. In front of you, on the tatami, lies a single roll of cloth.

The hall around you is a reconstructed room in the eh-doh-period show-in style, not any one building.

The word kimono is plain Japanese. Kee-roo, to wear. Moh-noh, a thing. A thing to wear.

So the question here may sound strange. Is a kimono a garment, or a set of rules?

Choose one now, and hold on to it. The rooms ahead will test your answer. Only the last one settles it.

Begin with the roll. Everything in the next room is made from it, and from nothing else.
