## Map

- **Satellite globe** (`tex/tex.js`, graded): NASA Blue Marble, "Land Surface, Shallow Water, and Shaded Topography" (2002), NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / NASA Earth Observatory. Public domain. [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Whole_world_-_land_and_oceans_12000.jpg)
- **Borders and coast** (`geo.js`, simplified): Natural Earth 1:110m and 1:50m admin-0 countries, 1:10m Thailand. Public domain. [naturalearthdata.com](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), via [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector).
- **The island and its three rivers** (`map.js`): a SIMPLIFIED DIAGRAM drawn for this deck from known geography (island ringed by the Chao Phraya to the west and south, the Lopburi to the north and the Pa Sak to the east, around 14.35°N 100.56°E). Not a survey map. The lower Chao Phraya, trade routes, flood and rice fields are approximate illustrations.

Buddhist-era years follow Thai school usage: founded 1893 BE (1351 CE), first fall 2112 BE, independence 2127 BE, Wat Chaiwatthanaram 2173 BE, fall 2310 BE (1767 CE), World Heritage 2534 BE (1991 CE). Slide 8 counts the years since 2310 BE from the computer's clock (259 in 2569 BE / 2026 CE).
