# LRCX (Lam Research) - PM brief, 2026-11-27

## Verdict
WATCH: a high-quality cyclical leader, down 42% in 6 months, but still 28x EBIT and a 2.1% FCF yield (2.7% normalized). Not yet a margin of safety.

## Business
Wafer fabrication equipment (deposition, etch, clean) plus customer support (spares, upgrades, services). FY26 (to 2026-06-28): systems $14.89B (+29.5%), customer support $8.35B (+20.2%). Equipment mix: foundry 54%, memory 39%, logic/IDM 7%. China 34% of revenue, Taiwan 22%, Korea 19% (10-K 0000707549-26-000037, Item 7).

## Moat
Real but cyclical. Customers qualify tools at high cost and tend to stay with a supplier, ROIC is 61% (XBRL screen), and about 36% of revenue is customer support. Against that: state-backed rivals may discount heavily, customers build some equipment, and the FY26 gross margin gain (50.5% vs 48.7%) was from customer mix, which can reverse.

## Key numbers (FY26)
- Revenue $23.23B (+26.0%); operating income about $8.20B (35.3% margin; my calculation from gross profit $11.73B less operating expenses $3.53B) (10-K, Item 7).
- Net income $7.27B; diluted EPS $5.76 vs $4.15 (10-K, Item 7).
- Operating cash flow $5.86B (down from $6.17B); capex $0.97B; FCF $4.89B, 21.1% margin vs 26.5% average (XBRL screen).
- Net cash of about $1.9B (net debt/FCF -0.38) (XBRL screen; my calculation). Share count down about 7% on the dilution measure (XBRL screen; period not in the excerpt).
- FY26 returned about $5.1B ($3.85B buybacks, $1.27B dividends) against $4.9B FCF (10-K, Item 7).
- Market cap $231B; EV/EBIT 28.0x (31.4x normalized, 1.33x its own history); FCF yield 2.1% (2.7% normalized) (XBRL screen).

## Why is it cheap?
It is not cheap in absolute terms. It is cheaper: last close $185.01, 1 month -23%, 6 months -42%, 5-year-high drawdown -57%, volatility 71% (price stats). The file gives no cause. Headlines are mostly positive on AI and memory demand. My reading is a de-rating on peak-cycle fears: FY26 margins and mix may be the high point, China is 34% of sales, memory timing is uneven, and FCF growth (1.5% CAGR) lags revenue growth (10.0% CAGR). The cyclical worry is valid, and the stock is still 33% above its EV/EBIT history. It is cheaper, not cheap.

## Cyclical check
The normalized screen gives a 2.66% FCF yield (about $6.1B of FCF, my calculation) and 31.4x EBIT (implying normalized EBIT near $7.3B, below FY26's $8.2B). The two normalizations disagree on direction, which points to working capital and capex timing that the excerpt cannot confirm.

## Disruption risk
Low to moderate: the 10-K warns that AI tools could reduce customers' reliance on Lam for process development (Item 1A).

## Top risks
1. Cyclical swings in demand with a hard-to-resize cost base.
2. Trade restrictions, tariffs and China exposure (34% of revenue).
3. State-backed and regional competitors, plus customer-built equipment.
4. Wrong technology bet or AI-driven shifts.
5. Rarely able to win back a line once a rival is qualified.

## Valuation
Composite score 0.676: valuation 0.22, momentum 0.89, quality 0.76, balance 0.86 (XBRL screen). The price assumes strong growth through the AI cycle, margins at or above FY26 and no hard China restriction. Shareholder yield is about 2.2% (my calculation).

## Thesis
- Leading deposition and etch franchise with switching costs and a service base.
- Net cash, 61% ROIC, buybacks shrinking the share count.
- AI-driven 3D scaling and advanced packaging raise equipment intensity; memory makers are reported to be spending again.
- Watchlist now: revisit if normalized EV/EBIT falls toward 22-24x or FCF yield exceeds about 3.5% with orders intact.

## What breaks the thesis
- Gross margin below 47% for two consecutive quarters (FY26: 50.5%).
- Quarterly revenue down more than 15% sequentially, or a guide-down citing memory or foundry delays.
- Trailing FCF margin below 15% (FY26: 21.1%), or operating cash flow trailing net income by more than $1.5B again (FY26: $5.86B vs $7.27B).
- China below 25% of revenue due to new export controls or a servicing ban.
- Deferred revenue below $2.0B (now $2.43B).
