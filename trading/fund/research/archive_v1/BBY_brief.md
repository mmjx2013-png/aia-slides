# BBY (Best Buy) - PM brief, 2026-11-27

## Verdict
WATCH. Good balance sheet and cash generation, but flat sales, a +41% six-month run, a multiple 1.38x above its own history, and a price above the one target seen (94.62). It is not cheap enough for a BUY now.

## What it is
US and Canada consumer electronics retailer: 1,068 stores, 34% online. Domestic mix: computing and phones 47%, consumer electronics 28%, appliances 11%, entertainment 7%, services 6% (10-K 0000764478-26-000009, Item 7). New profit streams: Best Buy Ads, Marketplace, memberships. Best Buy Health is being cut back after $171m of impairments.

## Key numbers (FY2026, ended 2026-01-31)
- Revenue $41,691m (+0.4%); comps +0.5% after -2.3% and -6.8% (10-K, Item 7).
- GAAP operating income $1,389m, 3.3% margin, after $190m restructuring and $171m impairments (10-K, Item 7). Domestic adjusted margin 4.4%.
- Gross margin 22.5% (flat). Diluted EPS $5.04 (10-K, Item 7).
- FCF $1,258m, 3.0% margin; 5-year average 2.5% (XBRL screen).
- Net cash (net debt/FCF -1.38x, net debt/EBITDA -0.78x); share count shrinking (dilution -6.0%) (XBRL screen).
- ROIC 89.5%, ROE 36.1% (XBRL screen); ROIC is flattered by a small capital base.
- Market cap $21.5bn. FCF yield 5.85%, normalized 4.77%. EV/EBIT 14.2x, normalized 13.7x, ratio to own history 1.377 (XBRL screen).
- Price 102.58 (2026-11-25): +4% 1m, +41% 6m, +43% 1y, 40% volatility, worst 5-year drawdown -44%.

## Moat
Narrow. Scale, vendor ties and services (Geek Squad, in-home) help it hold position, but flat gross margin and revenue below FY2024 show no pricing power. Vendors and carriers sell direct, and the service cost base is higher than some rivals (10-K, Item 1A).

## Why is it cheap (or was)?
The market feared stagnant, tariff-exposed, cyclical electronics demand and online and AI-agent disruption. Appliances (-8.9%) and home theater (-5.4%) still shrink. But computing and phones comped +5.7%, comps turned positive, and the company is net cash. The rerating has already happened, so it is only moderately cheap now. The worry was partly right; the stabilization is real but priced.

## Disruption
AI shopping agents and AI search could divert product discovery. Management is partnering on "agentic shopping" (10-K, Item 1A and 7).

## Top risks
1. Consumer electronics demand cycle and macro.
2. Tariffs and import dependence (IEEPA ruling 2026-02-20, refunds uncertain, new actions possible).
3. Competition and margin pressure from online, vendors, carriers and Marketplace sellers.
4. Technology and category cycles, inventory risk.
5. Execution on Ads, Marketplace and Health; recurring restructuring and labor costs.

## Valuation
5.8% FCF yield (4.8% normalized) and 13.7x to 14.2x EV/EBIT is fair for a no-growth retailer, and expensive against its own history. Price implies stable comps, margin about 3.3% GAAP, ongoing buybacks and a benign tariff outcome. A 6%+ normalized FCF yield (about $85 on these numbers, my arithmetic) would make it a BUY-candidate.

## Thesis (what would make us buy on a pullback)
- Net cash, falling share count and 5-6% FCF yield give a floor.
- Comps positive, led by computing and mobile.
- Mix shift to Ads, Marketplace and Services lifts margin without inventory (early; Services comps +1.0%).
- SG&A discipline (18.3% of revenue) continues.
- Entry price matters more than the story here.

## What breaks the thesis
- Comps negative for 2 consecutive quarters.
- Domestic adjusted operating margin below 4.0%.
- Gross margin below 22.0%.
- FCF margin below 2.0%.
- More than $200m of restructuring or impairment in a year, or net cash turning to net debt.

## Headlines (30 days)
Positive: Q3 FY2027 EPS beat, raised full-year outlook, analyst forecast increases, retail peers strong. One target (94.62) is below the market price. The Q3 numbers are not in the inputs, so the beat is unverified here.
