# MU brief for the portfolio manager (2026-11-27)

**Verdict: WATCH.** Peak-cycle earnings make it look cheap (EV/EBIT 6.5x, FCF yield 8.7%), but normalized it is 28x EV/EBIT and a 0.5% FCF yield. Not a BUY-candidate until we see evidence the new contracts keep margins high.

## Key numbers (FY2026, ended 2026-09-03; 10-K 0000723125-26-000023 and XBRL screen)
- Revenue $133.2B (+256%); gross margin 81% (FY25 40%); operating income $99.3B (75%); net income $85.0B.
- FCF $59.0B (44% margin); multi-year average FCF margin only 2.5%.
- Net cash about $35B (derived); net debt/EBITDA -0.32. Dilution 4.6% (share count rising).
- Price $601.25, market cap about $680B, 1-month -35%, 1-year +169%, volatility 87%.
- Normalized: FCF yield 0.48%, EV/EBIT 28.1x (XBRL screen).

## Business
Memory maker: DRAM (HBM, DDR5, LPDDR) $100.7B and NAND $31.8B. Segments: cloud memory 32%, core data center 28%, mobile and client 27%, automotive and embedded 12%. Data center is about 60% of revenue.

## Why it is cheap
The market treats FY26 as a peak. Growth was mostly price (DRAM ASP about +180%, NAND about +200%; bits only up mid-20%). The last two days brought a TrendForce DRAM price forecast cut, a supply glut warning, memory tariffs, a UBS target cut, a BNP downgrade and higher spending news. The market is partly right: memory has always reverted. The take-or-pay contracts (price floors, management says floor margins exceed past peaks) are the real counterargument, but they are new and unquantified in the excerpt.

## Moat
Oligopoly, process lead (1-gamma EUV DRAM, HBM4 in volume), strong balance sheet. Against: commodity pricing, and the profit came from price, not share.

## Cyclical view
Normalized EBIT is about $23B (derived), versus $99B this year. A middle case of $25-50B EBIT still gives 13-26x EV/EBIT. Priced in: profit at roughly one-third of the current level.

## Top risks
1. Price and margin reversal. 2. Supply glut as capacity and capex rise. 3. AI and data center dependence. 4. Contract durability. 5. Tariffs, Pillar Two tax, Singapore and Taiwan concentration. (Item 1A text was not in the excerpt; this is from MD&A, business text and headlines.)

## Thesis for a later BUY
- Contracts keep gross margin far above past troughs.
- AI demand keeps outrunning supply; HBM4 and HBM4E hold share.
- Net cash funds capex without leverage.
- Buy only at about a 10% FCF yield on a defensible mid-cycle FCF; look after the Dec 17 earnings.

## What breaks the thesis
- Gross margin under 60% for two quarters (81% now).
- DRAM or NAND prices down quarter on quarter for two quarters.
- Capex up while competitor supply accelerates.
- Contract disclosure showing small coverage or floors near past peak margins.
- A hyperscaler AI capex cut, or CMBU margin under 50%.

## Headlines
Heavily negative, with a roughly 7% one-day fall on 2026-11-25 and a -35% one-month return. Small positives: Tepper's top holding, and buy-the-dip calls before the Dec 17 earnings.
