# JBL (Jabil Inc) - PM brief, 2026-11-27

## Verdict
WATCH. A good business with strong returns, but not cheap against its own history, FY26 cash flow is helped by supplier-payment timing, and growth leans on concentrated AI infrastructure demand.

## What it is
Contract engineering and manufacturing for other companies (turnkey, so revenue is mostly pass-through parts). Segments FY26 (10-K 0001628280-26-067038, Item 7): Intelligent Infrastructure 50% of revenue (AI, cloud, networking; +45%), Regulated Industries 35% (auto, health, energy; +7%), Connected Living / Digital Commerce 15% (-4%).

## Key numbers (FY ended 2026-08-31)
- Revenue $35,954M, +20.6% (FY25 $29,802M; FY24 $28,883M) (10-K, Item 7).
- Gross margin 9.2%; operating income $1,704M, 4.7% margin (FY25 $1,182M; FY24 $2,013M) (10-K, Item 7; margin is my calculation).
- FCF $1,374M, 3.8% margin; 5-year-style average margin 3.25% (XBRL screen).
- Net debt about $2.1B (derived from net_debt_fcf 1.557 x FCF); net debt/EBITDA 0.90 (XBRL screen). Balance sheet is fine.
- ROIC 35.9% (XBRL screen). Share count shrinking: dilution -21.3% on the screen (window not stated); SBC $140M (10-K, Item 7).
- Price $228.14; market cap $23.49B; -37% in 6 months, -41% from 5-year high; volatility 50% a year.
- Working capital, quarter to 2026-08-31 vs a year earlier: days payable 135 vs 96, days inventory 82 vs 69, days receivable 55 vs 44, sales cycle 2 vs 18 days (10-K, Item 7).

## Why it is cheap (or not)
- It is not clearly cheap. EV/EBIT is 15.0 (14.2 normalized), about 1.39x its own history; normalized FCF yield is 5.0% (XBRL screen).
- The market worry: AI-capex dependence, a sharp analyst reset (Baird target $270 from $440), memory component shortage, and cash-flow quality.
- I think the worries are partly right. FY26 is probably near peak mix, and the payables stretch could reverse. The balance sheet and returns limit the downside.

## Cyclical view
Flagged cyclical. Normalized FCF is about $1.17B (3.25% x FY26 revenue, my calculation), not $1.37B. If operating margin fell back to the FY25 level of 4.0%, EBIT would be about $1.43B and EV/EBIT about 18x (my stress case).

## Top risks
1. Customers commit only about a quarter ahead and the cost base is fixed.
2. Customer concentration, rising with AI at 50% of revenue.
3. Component shortages (memory) and price pass-through lag.
4. Working capital and credit exposure to emerging customers.
5. Execution, technology change, and acquisitions.

## Thesis for a BUY, if it develops
- AI infrastructure keeps growing at about a 9% gross margin.
- FCF holds above about $1.2B after payables normalise.
- ROIC stays above 30% with the share count still falling.
- A lower entry price (normalized FCF yield above 5%) would help.

## What breaks the thesis
1. Gross margin below 8.5% for two consecutive quarters (FY26: 9.2%).
2. Trailing FCF below $1.0B, or days payable falling toward 96 without lower inventory.
3. Intelligent Infrastructure revenue turning negative year on year, or a top customer loss.
4. Days in inventory above 80 with write-downs.
5. Full-year operating margin below 4.0%.

## Headlines (30 days)
Dominated by analyst notes. Baird cut its target to $270 from $440 (negative), while the average target is quoted at $418, far above the price. The stock fell on 2026-11-24. Positive items (an India AI MoU, "undervalued" screens) are low-information. Nothing in the file explains the six-month fall. The JBL Charge 6 item is about the speaker brand, not Jabil.

## Size guidance
No position. Revisit after the next quarterly report shows FCF and payables, or at a materially lower price. If bought later, keep it well below the 8% cap given the 50% volatility.
