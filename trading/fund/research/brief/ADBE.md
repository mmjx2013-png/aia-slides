# ADBE (Adobe) - PM brief, 2026-11-27

**Verdict: WATCH** (leaning BUY-candidate on price). Very cheap on cash flow, but AI-disruption risk is unresolved and momentum is negative. Wait for evidence that ARR growth holds.

## Key numbers (FY ending 2025-11-28; 10-K 0000796343-26-000003 and XBRL screen)
- Revenue $23.77B, +11% (96% subscription). Digital Media $17.65B (+11%), Digital Experience $5.86B (+9%).
- Total ARR $25.20B, +11.5%. RPO $22.52B, +13%.
- Net income $7.13B (+28%). Operating cash flow $10.03B.
- FCF $9.85B, FCF margin 41.5% (average 39%). Operating margin about 36.6% and gross margin about 89% (derived from Item 7; operating income is not stated in the excerpt).
- Net debt about $0.8B (net debt/FCF 0.08). Share count shrinking (dilution -9.3%).
- Market cap $90.6B at $232.83. FCF yield 10.9% (normalized 10.2%). EV/EBIT 10.5x (normalized 11.2x), about 31% of its own history. Stock -27% over 1y, -62% over 3y, -66% from 5y high, 42% volatility.
- Not a cyclical name (cyclical_normalized = False).

## Why it is cheap
The market fears generative and agentic AI will commoditise creative and document tools and let AI-native entrants take low-end users, while AI inference costs squeeze margins. The 10-K names these risks (Item 1A). So far the reported numbers show no damage, so the worry looks partly priced in, but the filing has no data on seats, churn or price per seat. The long-run risk is real and unproven.

## Disruption risk
High: AI creation, editing and document tools compete directly with Creative Cloud and Acrobat.

## Top risks
1. AI competition and substitution, pressure on renewals and pricing.
2. AI cost margin squeeze (subscription cost of revenue +13% against revenue +12%).
3. AI regulation and IP liability (EU AI Act, third-party model licensing).
4. Brand backlash from the creative community.
5. Acquisition failure (the Figma deal ended with a $1B fee).

## Thesis
- About 10-11% FCF yield, almost no net debt, 41% FCF margin.
- Fundamentals intact: ARR +11.5%, RPO +13%, flat opex gives operating leverage.
- Buybacks shrink the share count.
- AI monetisation (Firefly, Acrobat AI Assistant, GenStudio) is free optionality.
- Expectations are low after a 66% drawdown.
- If upgraded to BUY, start small (2-3%), well below the 8% cap.

## What breaks the thesis
- Total ARR growth below 8% for two quarters (now 11.5%).
- Digital Media ARR or RPO growth below 8%.
- Operating margin below about 33% or gross margin below 87%.
- FCF margin below 35% for two quarters (now 41.5%).
- Price cuts or cheap AI bundling to defend share, or buybacks stop while the share count rises.

## Headlines (30 days)
Mostly earnings previews and stock-prediction pieces, neutral to mixed, with no hard news. Themes are AI competition, the freemium strategy and a valuation debate. The next earnings report is the catalyst to check against the breakers above. Stock-based compensation and the 10-Q (period 2026-08-28) figures are not in the filing excerpt.
